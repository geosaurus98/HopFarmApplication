# Possible additions

Ideas for later sections of the Nelson Lakes Hops app. Nothing here is built
yet. Each would be a new section on the home screen. Like Plant Health, it would
work offline and keep its own tab or sheet, with a CSV export for Excel.

Status: ☐ idea · ◐ waiting on Jason · ▶ in progress · ✔ done

## 1. Linked to mite monitoring (suggested first)

These two fit best with what's already built. Releases and sprays could show as
markers on the existing **Trend by block** chart. Then you could see at a glance
whether PP counts rose after a release, or whether a miticide knocked back
predators as well as two-spotted mites.

| | Addition | Records | Needs from Jason |
|---|---|---|---|
| ◐ | **Predator release log** | Date, block, product or supplier, quantity released, cost | Products and suppliers used; units (bottles, per ha?) |
| ▶ | **Spray diary** — plan in [spray-diary-plan.md](spray-diary-plan.md); also covers predator releases and fertiliser | Date, block, product, rate, water volume, operator, weather, target pest | What his compliance or export programme requires on a spray record; product list; whether withholding periods should be tracked |

## 2. Season and harvest

| | Addition | Records | Needs from Jason |
|---|---|---|---|
| ☐ | **Harvest and yield** | Picking date per block, wet and dry weight, kiln batch, moisture, bale numbers, yield per ha (worked out from block area) | How weights and bales are recorded now; per block or per variety |
| ☐ | **Kiln / drying log** | Batch, block, temperatures, duration, final moisture | What a buyer asks for when tracing a batch |

These would link to the mite and plant health data by block and season, so that
over time you can see which blocks perform and why.
